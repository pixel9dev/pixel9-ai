**Telolexic Audit Protocol (TAP)** | **Udimudi Naga Raju** | July 2026

## The Opportunity

Enterprises are deploying generative AI faster than they can **trust** it. Hallucinations cost credibility. Video continuity errors cost production time. Agent errors cost security.

**TAP is the missing governance layer** — a model-agnostic protocol that audits AI outputs **backward from the terminal state** for causal integrity.

---

## The Problem (30 seconds)

Every major generative system runs **forward**: next token, next pixel, next frame. That design optimizes creation, not verification. The result:

- 15%+ of fluent LLM outputs contain logical discontinuities invisible to surface metrics  
- AI video tools produce object morphing, disappearing props, and physics violations  
- Autonomous agents lack explainable pre-commit audit gates  

**Regulation (EU AI Act), enterprise governance, and media authenticity demands are converging on auditable AI.**

---

## The Solution (30 seconds)

**TAP anchors the end state and audits backward.**

1. Lock terminal truth \(S_n\)  
2. Traverse states in reverse  
3. Flag missing precursors (continuity violation) or unexplained new elements (exigenesis)  

Works across **text, images, video, code, cryptography, and agents** — without retraining foundation models.
